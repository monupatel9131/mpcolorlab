About MP Color Lab

Explore. Create. Convert. Master Colors.

MP Color Lab is a free online collection of practical color utilities for students, designers, developers, content creators, website owners and digital marketers. Many tools are intentionally browser-based, allowing calculations and image color processing to happen locally without requiring a server.

MP Color Lab is a project by MP Digital Hub. The goal is to provide useful, lightweight utilities with clear results, practical copy/export options and an interface that works well across phones, tablets and desktops.

Legal & Information

Clear, Simple Policies

Important information about using MP Color Lab.

Privacy Policy

MP Color Lab is designed to minimize unnecessary data handling. Most color calculations happen directly in the browser. Image-based tools in this implementation process selected images locally with the Canvas API and do not upload them to a server.

Favorites and recent color history may be stored in your browser's localStorage. The current implementation does not require an account or collect passwords, OTPs or payment information.

The current project does not intentionally include third-party analytics or advertising scripts. If analytics, advertisements, external APIs or other third-party services are added in a future version, the relevant privacy disclosures should be updated.
